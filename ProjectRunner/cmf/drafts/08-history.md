Phantom Break Inc., operating as Phantom Break Studio, holds 100% of the rights to Project Runner, including its story, characters, world, designs, code and other media. Michael Brandt is the company's sole shareholder and director. All concepts and prototype work made in the Prototyping Program will be the full property of Phantom Break Inc.

### How the project came about

Michael Brandt wrote the first version of the concept, then called Project Echo, in [month year]. The concept document of June 2026 describes a narrative-driven cyberpunk adventure. The player is Chase Roberts, a Cyber Runner who repairs and fits cyberware from an underground clinic in NoWhere, a lawless city also called The Hive. It set out the ideas Project Runner keeps: timed dialogue choices whose results carry through the game, hacking, and gangs that profit from the clinic's trade.

On 2026-10-01 the studio turned the concept into Project Runner, a narrative game in five episodes made of side-on scenes. Development began in an existing Unreal Engine 5.8 test project built on Epic's Game Animation Sample, which already had characters and a link to the studio's Houdini tools. Within the first week a greybox scene played from start to finish. It has walking, an automatic crouch zone, objects to look at and use, a hold-to-hack device, a conversation with a timed choice, and a cutscene that changes with the choice. The team then set the HD-2D look (2026-10-08) and wrote a five-episode story outline (2026-10-15).

The scene runs on two plugins Phantom Break wrote for this project, PhantomInteraction and PhantomStory. The studio will reuse them in a later game, ProjectSilence.
