## What the signer declares

1. They are an officer of the applicant and authorized to make the declaration.
2. They have done full due diligence and know the facts personally.
3. They have read the CMF Narrative Positioning Policy, and the applicant will comply with it in every phase of the project.
4. The CMF relies on the declaration to decide eligibility.
5. A false declaration is a CMF Event of Default and may be a criminal offence.

Signing it is also an applicant eligibility condition (CORE p.6 s.3.1). There is one form per applicant; a co-applicant would need its own. Project Runner has no co-applicant.
