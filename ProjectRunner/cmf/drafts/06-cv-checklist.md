## Rules every CV must follow

From the Shred review (2024) and Shred's best-practice sheet:

1. Personal information at the top: full name, email, phone, mailing address.
2. Professional summary next, as one prose paragraph, then a separate bulleted skills list. Both go above work history, education and awards.
3. Work history, most recent first. The first entry is the person's role at Phantom Break on Project Runner. Each entry has company or project, role, and from-to dates. Ongoing roles say "2019 to present", not just a colour or "current".
4. Show work with other team members (shared projects, same employer) without naming them. The team description makes the link.
5. Education, certifications and training, with institution, place, credential and dates.
6. Awards and nominations, with name, date and a link if there is one.
7. 3 pages at most. Same section titles and the same bullet and prose choices in every CV.
8. CMF format rules: simple font over 11 pt, black on white, no columns (RD p.2).
