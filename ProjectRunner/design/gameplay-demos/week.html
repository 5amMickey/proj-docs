Week demo

Goal: can you see where your time goes, and is it fun to choose who gets it when you can't do everything? This is one episode with placeholder patients. Three quick keys stand in for each surgery.

Specialty

Numbers

The rules

Each dot is an action. Cyan dots are yours to spend, and amber dots are already set aside for a story patient. Treating a patient, breaking down gear, asking around and sending a contact each use a dot, and a story patient takes 2. Paying the Hacker Contact uses materials and no dot. A bar shows the days a patient will wait. If it ends with them untreated, they leave and you lose XP. A level never drops, and each one gives one upgrade.

Controls

The d-pad or left stick picks a patient, in any direction. Choices use the buttons shown. Controller: A, B, X, Y. Keyboard: WASD or the arrow keys to pick, E for A, 1 for X, 2 for Y, 3 for B.
