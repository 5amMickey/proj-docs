Gameplay demos

Local test pages for the ProjectRunner gameplay proposal. They use placeholder shapes and placeholder lines, and nothing here is approved. Each is done when you have played it and ruled to keep, change or cut that system.

  • Treatment: does the surgery build tension and lead you to find the malware yourself? It now runs by itself after a scene setup.
  • QTE fight: does a fight read and feel right with one key on screen and a hold as the finisher?
  • Dialogue, fight and final choice: does the talk hold together through the quick hack, the chair fight and the last choice with Vhoori?
  • Fighter case: kept for reference only. The Treatment demo has moved past it, and it still uses the old hold bar and reputation.
  • Week: can you see where your time goes, and is it fun to choose who gets it?
  • Ring hack levels: approve each level, its new mechanic and its difficulty.
  • Ring hack, custom: every setting on one panel, for tuning.
