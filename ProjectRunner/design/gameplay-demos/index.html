Gameplay demos

Local test pages for the ProjectRunner gameplay proposal. They use placeholder shapes and placeholder lines, and nothing here is approved. Each is done when you have played it and ruled to keep, change or cut that system.

  • Treatment: does the surgery build tension and lead you to find the malware yourself?
  • QTE fight: does a fight read and feel right with one key on screen and a hold as the finisher?
  • Dialogue and spec option: can you keep answering on X, Y and B during a quick hack?
  • Fighter case: does the whole loop make sense without explanation?
  • Ring hack: is the search step fun, and which layers stay?
