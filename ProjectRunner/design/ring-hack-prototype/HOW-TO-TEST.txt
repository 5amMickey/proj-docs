Ring hack test

You need a computer, Chrome or Edge, and ideally a game controller.

1. Unzip this folder, then open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
2. Connect your controller and press a button on it. A keyboard and mouse also work.
3. Type your name at the end of the side panel if you want it on your results. This is optional.
4. Click Start and play at least five locks. Leave the settings alone unless you've been asked to change them.
5. When a lock ends, an end screen shows how you did. Rate how hard it was, then press Reset for the next lock.
6. After your last lock, click Download results on the end screen, and send that file back.

The page records each lock you play: how it ended, how long it took, how many mistakes you made, your rating and your settings. It also records your controller's name and the name you typed. It stays in your browser until you download it, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Controls

Controller: right stick to search, R1 to fit, d-pad or left stick for arrows. On the end screen, X, Y and B rate the lock and A resets it.
Keyboard and mouse: mouse or left and right arrows to search, E to fit, WASD or arrow keys for arrows. On the end screen, 1, 2 and 3 rate the lock and Enter resets it.
